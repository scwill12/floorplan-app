Floorplan Support

Need help with Floorplan: Measure & Draw? Email colenegri53@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I change a wall's length? Tap the wall (or any dimension) and type the new length, like 11'3 or 135. The red pin shows which end stays put; tap Flip to swap.

Why do my measurements say they're off? When every side of a room is typed, Floorplan checks that they close. Re-measure the highlighted wall, or tap Adjust to close.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings › Purchases › Restore Purchases.

Where are my plans stored? On your device, and in your private iCloud if you turn on iCloud Sync in Settings (takes effect next launch).
