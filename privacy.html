Floorplan Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Floorplan: Measure & Draw ("Floorplan") is made by Cole Williams. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Floorplan has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run servers that receive your data.

Your plans

Plans are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, they are stored in your private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. That data is held by Apple under your Apple Account; we cannot see it.

When you export or share a plan (image, PDF, DXF, CSV or .floorplan file), it goes only where you send it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple to unlock Pro features.

Children

Floorplan does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
